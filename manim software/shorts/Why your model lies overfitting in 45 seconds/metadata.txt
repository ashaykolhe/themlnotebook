Overfitting = your model memorised the training data, not the patterns.

Quick cheat sheet:
- Overfit = high train accuracy, low test accuracy
- Fix 1: get more training data
- Fix 2: add L1 or L2 regularisation
- Fix 3: simplify your model (fewer layers / prune the tree)
