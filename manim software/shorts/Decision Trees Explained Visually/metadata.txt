💡 The key formula:
Gini = 1 − (p_yes² + p_no²)
→ 0.0 means a perfectly pure node (all one class)
→ 0.5 means maximum chaos (50/50 mix)

The tree always picks the split that lowers Gini the most.

🔁 Decision Trees are the base learner for:
→ Random Forest (bagging many trees)
→ XGBoost / LightGBM (boosting trees sequentially)

Understanding this one algorithm unlocks most of modern ML.
