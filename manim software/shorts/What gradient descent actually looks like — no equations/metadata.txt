Enjoyed this? Here's what's actually happening in the animation:

🔵  The curve = loss surface (how wrong the model is at every setting)
🟠  The ball = your model's current parameters
⬇️  Each step = gradient descent updating the weights
🟢  The star = global minimum (best possible model)
🔴  The trap = local minimum (looks like the bottom, isn't)

The "learning rate" controls step size — too big and it bounces forever, too small and it takes forever to converge.

This is literally what happens inside ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, and every neural network you've ever used.
